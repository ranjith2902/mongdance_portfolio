MONG

Founder | Choreographer
Dance Educator | Performer

I am a passionate and dynamic choreographer with 6 years of experience in choreography and over 14 years of experience as a dancer. Over the years, I have created and directed dance routines for a wide range of events, stage performances, classes, projects, and visual productions.

My approach to choreography combines creativity, storytelling, musicality, and performance energy to create engaging and impactful experiences for audiences. I enjoy working across different styles and concepts, always focusing on bringing originality, precision, and emotion into every performance.

Alongside choreography, I have experience collaborating with dancers, creative teams, and directors to ensure smooth execution and strong stage presentation. I strive to create performances that are visually compelling, meaningful, and memorable for both performers and audiences alike.

Beyond performance and choreography, I am deeply passionate about teaching dance as an art form. I believe dance is not only about movement, but also about expression, discipline, confidence, and individuality. I strive to create a positive and inspiring learning environment where students can grow creatively, build self-confidence, and develop a genuine love for dance.

Film & Media Works

Worked as an assistant choreographer alongside leading choreographers in the film industry, television industry and Digital Medias contributing to the creative and executional aspects of film songs, promotional shoots, and media productions. Gained valuable experience in choreography planning, artist coordination, rehearsal management, and on-set performance execution within professional production environments.
